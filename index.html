THE HERD

Goats CZ (GCZ) is a premier Valorant team built on aggressive plays, tactical superiority, and an unbreakable bond. Formed by a group of High Immortal and Radiant prodigies, our journey started in the local Czech ladders. As a purely Czech roster, we are dominating the domestic scene with our unique, fearless playstyle.

Led by our IGL Dejwrix, we don't just play the game; we redefine the meta. From flawless executes to unbreakable site holds, the 'Herd' operates as a single, lethal unit. Our eyes are set on the highest tiers of European Valorant, and we won't stop until we reach the top.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are you looking for new players? +
Our core roster and subs are currently locked in for the Premier season. However, we always keep an eye out for exceptional High Immortal/Radiant Czech talent to join our practice squad.
Who does the in-game calling? +
Dejwrix is our dedicated IGL (In-Game Leader), orchestrating the mid-round calls, utility combinations, and overall match strategy.
How often do you practice? +
We play official Premier matches weekly and run internal scrims against other top-tier Contender teams 3-4 times a week to stay sharp.
Do you have a dedicated coach? +
Currently, our IGL and the core team handle VOD reviews and strategy crafting. However, we are open to working with an experienced coach in the future.
How long have you been playing together? +
The core of the team has been grinding together since this year (2026), building deep synergy and absolute trust in each other's plays.
What are the ultimate goals of the team? +
Our primary objective is to dominate the Premier Contender division and fight our way into the Challengers league through Ascension.
Where can I watch your official matches? +
We stream most of our official Premier matches and tournaments directly on our player POVs via Twitch and Kick. Follow our Discord to get notified when we go live!
How can I support Goats CZ? +
The best way to support us right now is by joining our Discord, following our socials, and cheering for us in stream chats during match days. You can also support the team directly by donating here. Merch is also dropping soon!
I have more questions. How can I contact you? +
If you have any other questions, business inquiries, or just want to say hi, feel free to drop us an email at goatscz0@gmail.com (Click to copy).
